# QA rubric

Score 1–5. A 1–2 in any dimension blocks release. Weighted result must be at least 85/100.

- **Strategy — 15%:** specific audience, problem, promise, proof, and action.
- **Brand coherence — 15%:** message, voice, color, type, imagery, and CTA remain traceable across channels.
- **Visual hierarchy — 10%:** attention order is intentional; density and whitespace support comprehension.
- **Channel adaptation — 10%:** output uses each format's behavior rather than simple resizing.
- **Accessibility — 15%:** contrast, legibility, captions, focus, semantics, reduced motion, and safe areas.
- **Truth and compliance — 15%:** claims, prices, dates, disclaimers, regulated review, and sources are verified.
- **Rights and privacy — 10%:** licenses, releases, confidential data, credentials, and personal data are controlled.
- **Technical quality — 5%:** responsive states, errors, exports, links, and playback work.
- **Conversion clarity — 5%:** primary CTA is obvious, credible, measurable, and consistent.

## Severity

- **Blocker:** privacy breach, credential exposure, missing regulated approval, unlawful/deceptive claim, or unlicensed critical asset.
- **Critical:** broken primary journey, unreadable mobile output, inaccessible required action, or contradictory promise/CTA.
- **Major:** visible inconsistency, weak proof, wrong export, or unsupported secondary claim.
- **Minor:** polish issue with no material effect on trust, comprehension, access, or action.

## Iteration record

For each loop, record: observation, evidence, severity, change, expected effect, regression risk, verification, approver.
